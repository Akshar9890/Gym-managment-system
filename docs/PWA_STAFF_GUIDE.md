# PWA_STAFF_GUIDE.md — BSF THE GYM App Installation & Offline Operations Guide

This guide is designed for gym front-desk staff, floor managers, and administrators. Follow these instructions to install the **BSF THE GYM** app on your phone, tablet, or front-desk computer, and learn how to manage day-to-day operations during network drops.

---

## 1. Why Install the BSF Gym App?

Installing BSF THE GYM as a Progressive Web App (PWA) gives you:
- **1-Tap Launch**: Launches directly from your home screen or desktop taskbar like a native app.
- **Full Screen Workspace**: No browser search bars, tabs, or accidental closures.
- **Faster Speeds**: Core files, gym logos, and screens are saved locally on your device for lightning-fast loading.
- **Offline Protection**: If gym Wi-Fi or cellular service fluctuates, the app protects against lost data and shows clear reconnection steps.

---

## 2. Installation Instructions by Device

### A. Android Phones & Tablets (Google Chrome)

#### Automatic Install Banner:
1. Open Google Chrome and navigate to the gym portal URL (e.g. `https://bsf-gym.example.com`).
2. An amber **"Install BSF THE GYM"** banner will appear near the bottom right of the screen.
3. Tap **"Install App"**.
4. Confirm by tapping **"Install"** on the Android system popup.
5. The **BSF GYM** icon will appear on your home screen and in your app drawer.

#### Manual Install (if banner was dismissed):
1. Tap the **three dots menu (⋮)** in the top right corner of Google Chrome.
2. Select **"Install app"** or **"Add to Home screen"**.
3. Confirm the prompt by tapping **"Install"**.

---

### B. Apple iPhone & iPad (Safari)

> [!NOTE]
> On iOS, installation **must** be done through Apple Safari.

1. Open **Safari** and visit the gym portal URL.
2. Log in or view the login screen.
3. At the bottom of the Safari screen (or top on iPad), tap the **Share** button (the square icon with an arrow pointing upward).
4. Scroll down the options list and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
5. You will see the BSF THE GYM icon and title. Tap **"Add"** in the top-right corner.
6. The **BSF GYM** app icon will now appear on your iPhone/iPad home screen. Tap it to launch in full-screen standalone mode.

---

### C. Front-Desk Workstation (Windows PC / macOS / ChromeOS)

#### Using Google Chrome:
1. Open Chrome and navigate to the gym portal.
2. Look at the right side of the address bar (where the website URL is displayed).
3. Click the **Install Icon** (a small desktop screen with a down arrow: `⊕` / `⤓`).
4. Click **"Install"** on the confirmation box.
5. *(Optional)* Right-click the app icon in your Windows Taskbar or macOS Dock and select **"Pin to Taskbar"** / **"Keep in Dock"**.

#### Using Microsoft Edge:
1. Open Edge and navigate to the gym portal.
2. Look at the right side of the address bar and click the **"App available. Install BSF THE GYM"** icon.
3. Click **"Install"**.

## 5. Offline Mode: Rules & Staff Guidelines

The app includes built-in offline intelligence to keep you informed when the gym internet disconnects.

### Visual Status Indicators
- **Offline Alert**: If your connection drops, an amber status pill appears at the bottom center:
  > ⚠️ `Offline Mode — Cached pages available`
- **Reconnected Notice**: When internet returns, a green pill confirms:
  >  `Connection Restored`

### Offline Do's and Don'ts

| Feature | Works Offline? | Staff Instructions |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Viewing Previously Loaded Pages** |  **YES** | You can review screens you already opened (e.g. member details, staff dashboard). |
| **Recording Payments** | ❌ **BLOCKED** | **Do NOT collect new payment records without active connection.** The system blocks offline payment recording to prevent double charges and duplicate receipts. |
| **New Member Registration** | ❌ **BLOCKED** | Must be performed with an active connection to ensure phone numbers and sequential IDs are verified. |
| **Payment Proof Uploads** | ❌ **BLOCKED** | Image uploads require internet to transmit to the server. |
| **Password / Passkey Changes** | ❌ **BLOCKED** | Security credentials require live server verification. |

### How to Reconnect
If you navigate to a page that hasn't been cached, you will see the **BSF THE GYM Offline Screen**:
1. Check that the front-desk Wi-Fi router is turned on, or switch your device to mobile data.
2. Tap the amber button: **"Check & Reconnect"**.
3. Once internet is detected, the screen will reload your dashboard automatically.

## 6. How App Updates Work

You never need to visit an App Store or Play Store to update the BSF Gym app:
- Every time you open the app, it checks for updates silently in the background.
- When a software update is deployed, the app will refresh automatically to load the newest version.
- If a new version is waiting, you can close and re-open the app, or swipe down to pull-to-refresh.

## 7. Troubleshooting & FAQ

#### Q1: "The camera is not opening when capturing payment proof inside the app."
- **Solution**: Ensure camera permissions are allowed.
  - **Android**: Long-press the BSF GYM app icon → App Info → Permissions → Camera → Allow.
  - **iOS**: Settings → Safari → Camera → Allow, or Settings → BSF GYM → Camera → Allow.

#### Q2: "I accidentally tapped 'Not Now' on the install banner."
- **Solution**: The banner pauses for your current session so it doesn't distract you. You can still install the app at any time via your browser menu (Chrome: `⋮` → "Install app"; Safari: Share → "Add to Home Screen").

#### Q3: "I made changes, but the screen looks outdated."
- **Solution**: Perform a hard refresh:
  - **Desktop**: Press `Ctrl + Shift + R` (Windows) or `Cmd + Shift + R` (Mac).
  - **Mobile**: Pull down from the very top of the screen to refresh.
